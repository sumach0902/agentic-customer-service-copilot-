## Complaints

Any expression of dissatisfaction, or a request for a manager or supervisor, must be treated as a complaint and handed to a trained human agent with full context. The automated agent must not argue, minimise the complaint, or offer compensation.

## Vulnerable customers

Customers facing financial hardship, bereavement, or other vulnerability must be offered a human agent and extra time, not automated demands or rushed answers. The agent should acknowledge the situation calmly and hand off with context.

## Ticket status meanings

In Progress means the request is being worked on. Waiting for customer means we need information from the customer before we can continue. Completed and Resolved mean the work is finished and confirmed.

## Service level

Most standard requests are completed within 5 working days. Card delivery requests are normally completed within 7 working days from dispatch.

## Updates

Customers should receive a status update whenever a ticket changes status, and a next-step message explaining what happens next and by when.

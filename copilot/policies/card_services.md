## Card delivery

A replacement card is normally dispatched within 2 working days and delivered within 7 working days from dispatch. Customers can track delivery through their ticket status.

## Card replacement

Card replacement is an account action that requires identity verification by a human agent. The demo agent explains the process and prepares a handoff, but does not order a replacement card itself.

## Lost or stolen cards

If a card is lost or stolen, the customer should report it immediately to a human agent so the card can be blocked. Do not delay a lost-card report with automated guidance.

## Savings account fees

The savings account has no monthly maintenance fee. There is no fee for online transfers between your own accounts, and no fee for viewing statements online.

## Interest rate

Interest on savings accounts is calculated daily and credited monthly. The current rate is published on the product page and may change with notice. The agent must quote only the current published rate, never a rate from an older document.

## Withdrawal limits

Savings accounts allow up to 6 withdrawals per month without a fee. Additional withdrawals in the same month may attract a fee, which is shown before the withdrawal is confirmed by an agent.

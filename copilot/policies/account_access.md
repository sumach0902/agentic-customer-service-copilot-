## Verification

Before sharing account details, verify the customer's identity using the account ID and one registered contact detail. Never share another customer's account information, even if the caller provides a name.

## Account status

An account shows as Active, Restricted, or Closed. Only Active accounts can use online services. If an account is Restricted, the customer must speak to an agent to resolve the restriction.

## Recent activity

Customers can view their recent activity for the last 90 days. Activity older than 90 days requires an agent to retrieve it from archived records.

## Payment due date

Monthly payments are due on the 5th of each month. If the 5th falls on a weekend or public holiday, the payment is due on the next working day.

## Late payment

A payment made after the due date may attract a late fee, and the account may show a late-payment notice for that cycle. Customers in financial difficulty should be offered a human agent, not an automated payment demand.

## Payment methods

Customers can pay by bank transfer, card, or direct debit. The agent in this demo is read-only and does not take payments, refunds, or transfers. Those actions require a human agent.

## Privacy

Only show a customer their own records. Mask account numbers, emails, and phone numbers in any answer or handoff summary. Never include another customer's data in an answer, a log shown to a customer, or a training example.

## Audit

Every agent answer, tool call, and handoff must be logged with the question, the sources used, and the outcome, so answers can be reviewed and corrected later.

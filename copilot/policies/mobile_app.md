## Mobile app login

Customers can sign in with their registered email and password, plus a one-time code sent to their registered phone. If login fails three times, the account is temporarily locked and the customer should use password reset or speak to an agent.

## Product guidance scope

Product guidance covers how a product works, its features, fees, and limits, as described in current approved documents. It does not include personal financial advice, and it must not recommend a product the customer has not asked about.
